    We are a design and tech studio

    Rumi Byte builds brands that look sharp and websites that feel alive. From the first logo sketch to the last ad report, we handle the whole journey.

    We cover the identity, the user experience, the website, and then the ads and content that bring people to it. Because design, development and marketing sit with one team, nothing gets lost between them.

    Every project gets the same care: clear communication, honest timelines and work we are proud to put our name on.

    Meet the founder

    Umme Ruman

    Founder & Creative Lead, Rumi Byte

    Hi, I'm Ruman. I design brands, build websites and run the ads that help them grow.

    I started my journey in freelancing with a simple goal: help businesses look as good online as they really are. Along the way I learned graphic design, UI/UX, website design and development, and Meta advertising, and I realised the best results come when all of these work together.

    That idea became Rumi Byte. When you work with me, the person who designs your logo also understands your website and your ad campaigns, so your brand stays consistent everywhere your customers see it.

    I'm young, curious and always learning, and I bring that energy to every project. I listen carefully, keep you updated at every step and don't stop until the work feels right.
