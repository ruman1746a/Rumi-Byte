      About Rumi Byte Est. 2026 · Remote studio

      Rumi Byte is a design and technology studio. We help ambitious businesses look world-class, launch websites that work as hard as they do, and reach the people who matter, wherever they are in the world.

      Most businesses juggle a designer, a developer and a marketer who never talk to each other. We bring all three under one roof, so your brand, website and campaigns are planned together and speak with one voice.

      We work remotely with clients across time zones, communicate in clear English, and keep every project organised from the first call to launch day and beyond.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      Everything clients usually ask before starting a project. Can’t find yours? Send us a message.

      What does Rumi Byte do?

      Rumi Byte is a remote design and technology studio. We create brand identities, graphic design, UI/UX design, websites, Shopify stores and AI automations, and we grow brands with Meta ads, TikTok ads, social media management, SEO, content writing and video editing.

      Do you work with international clients?

      Yes. We work remotely with businesses around the world, including the USA, the UK, Qatar, the UAE and Europe. We schedule calls around your time zone and communicate in clear, professional English.

      How much does a project cost?

      Every project is different, so we quote after a free intro call. You get a clear, fixed quote and a project plan before any work begins, with no hidden costs.

      How long does it take to build a website?

      It depends on the size of the project. A brand identity or a small business website usually takes a few weeks, and you receive a clear timeline in your project plan before we start.

      Can you redesign my existing website or brand?

      Yes. We can refresh your current logo and visuals, redesign your website for a modern, mobile-first experience, or rebuild it completely while keeping what already works.

      Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

      Yes. Every website we build is responsive, fast and set up with on-page SEO basics such as clean structure, page titles, descriptions and structured data, so it is easier to find on Google and AI search tools.

      Do you offer support after launch?

      Yes. After launch we can manage your ads, social media, SEO and content, track results and send you easy-to-read reports, so your brand keeps growing.

      How do I get started?

      Send us a message on WhatsApp at +92 303 7957116 or email rumibyte@gmail.com with a few details about your business. We will book a free intro call and suggest the best next step.
