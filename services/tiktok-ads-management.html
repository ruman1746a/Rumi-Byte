Questions

Do I need to appear on camera?

Not always. We can plan ideas using products, text, voiceovers or user-style content.

Is TikTok right for my business?

If your customers are young or your product is visual, TikTok can be a strong channel. We will advise honestly.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Rumi Byte works remotely with businesses in the USA, the UK, Qatar, the UAE and around the world. We schedule calls around your time zone and communicate in clear, professional English.

How much does it cost?

Pricing starts from $299 per month for international clients and PKR 16,500 per month in Pakistan (ad budget is separate). You receive a clear, fixed quote and a project plan after a free intro call, before any work begins.
